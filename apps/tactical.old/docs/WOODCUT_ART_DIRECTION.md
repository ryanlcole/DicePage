# Woodcut Art Direction

Milestone: `SHAELVIEN-WOODCUT-TILESET-1`

Shaelvien exploration maps use an original handcrafted visual language based on burned wood, engraved atlas linework, and tabletop map pieces.

The image should read as carved first and colored second.

## Visual Pillars

- Warm oak and walnut bases.
- Charcoal-burned outlines.
- Engraved grain, contour, and hatching marks.
- Muted moss, blue, stone, ash, parchment, cedar, and earth accents.
- Strong silhouettes readable at 32x32.
- Nearest-neighbor scaling.

## Avoid

- Photoreal textures.
- Glossy materials.
- Modern bloom or particle-style VFX.
- Commercial sprite references.
- Palette imitation from existing RPGs.
- Unregistered art files.

## Animation

Animation is presentation-only. It must never affect gameplay state, replay hashes, action validation, or simulation timing.

Current restrained overlays:

- Water shimmer line overlay.
- Rune/fire/portal pulse rings.
