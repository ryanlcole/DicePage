Accessibility Statement

Last updated August 24, 2026.

PrivacyTermsSafety

RIST WORLD is being built with the goal of supporting accessible tabletop play across phones, computers, keyboards, touch, and future controller-based play.

Target

The project is working toward practical conformance with WCAG 2.2 Level AA where applicable. Because RIST WORLD is an active alpha, the site does not claim complete conformance yet.

Current design goals

  • Keyboard-accessible controls and visible focus states.
  • Touch targets that do not require precise pointer control.
  • Text alternatives and meaningful labels for functional imagery.
  • Readable contrast and scalable text.
  • Navigation that does not depend solely on dragging.
  • Support for reduced motion where animation is introduced.
  • Clear error messages and form labels.
  • Controller-friendly play-mode navigation as the interface develops.

Report an accessibility barrier

If a feature is difficult or impossible to use because of an accessibility barrier, email relic.gamemaster@gmail.com. Include the page or feature, device/browser, and what you were trying to do. Alternative access or a repair will be considered where reasonably possible.
