Safety & TAKE IT DOWN Requests

Last updated August 24, 2026.

PrivacyTermsCopyright / DMCA
To request removal of a nonconsensual intimate image or video, including an AI-generated or digitally altered image, email relic.gamemaster@gmail.com with the subject “TAKE IT DOWN Request.”

What to include

Give enough information to identify the content and where it appears on RIST WORLD, such as the page, account, message, card, asset, file, or URL. State that the depicted person did not consent to the intimate content being shared. Include a reliable way to contact you so the request can be acknowledged and tracked.

What RIST WORLD will do

Valid requests concerning covered nonconsensual intimate imagery will be prioritized. RIST WORLD will seek to remove the reported content and make reasonable efforts to identify and remove known identical copies within 48 hours of receiving a valid request, as required where the TAKE IT DOWN Act applies. A request may be acknowledged with a reference number and the requester may be told when action is completed or why a request could not be validated.

No account required

You do not need a RIST WORLD account to submit a TAKE IT DOWN request.

Other urgent safety reports

Use the same contact address for credible threats, exploitation of minors, doxxing, impersonation, malicious uploads, or other urgent platform-safety concerns. Put “URGENT SAFETY” in the subject line when immediate review is appropriate.

Preservation and law enforcement

Content may be restricted while a report is reviewed. Information may be preserved or disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect users, or respond to valid legal process.

Contact

relic.gamemaster@gmail.com

This process is intended to provide clear platform notice and reporting while RIST WORLD remains in alpha. It may be expanded with in-app reporting and automated duplicate-detection tools.
