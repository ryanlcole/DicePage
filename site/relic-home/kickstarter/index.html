Current product facts

What exists now

Public alpha

RIST WORLD is under active development and deployed as a browser-first application.

Server authority

World authority, account entitlements, property claims, and commerce grants are enforced by the AWS authority service rather than browser state.

Persistent world structure

The current world model uses stable World IDs, planes, cubes, tiers, layers, regions, and spatial content.

Shaelvien property

The current MMO claim lattice expands from Endemar. A claimed property space is 2048 × 2048 pixels with a maximum height of 100 layers.

Genesis token

Completing a RIST profile creates one server-held Shaelvien Token that can be spent on an eligible property-space claim.

Complimentary access

The authority service supports one-use, expiring, revocable access links so selected people can receive plan access without payment.

Kickstarter facts

What can be stated before launch

Kickstarter states that successfully funded U.S. projects pay a 5% platform fee. Its U.S. fee page currently lists ordinary payment processing at 3% + $0.30 per pledge, with a separate micropledge schedule for pledges under $10.

Kickstarter states that it does not take equity in projects. Kickstarter is rewards-based crowdfunding; a pledge is not an equity investment in ReLiCGameMaster.

The project has not yet published a Kickstarter funding goal, expected backer count, conversion rate, or campaign revenue projection. Those values are intentionally not invented here.
