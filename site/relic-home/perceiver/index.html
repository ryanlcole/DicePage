Noise inclusion: the microphone is analysis-only and is never connected to speaker output. Browser acoustic echo cancellation is requested, while noise suppression and automatic gain control are left off so Perceiver can measure the room instead of trying to erase it.

Distance audio: bass receives the least distance attenuation, mids more, and treble the most. Room-matched boosts are deliberately bounded before a separate dynamics limiter and digital headroom governor.

Hearing safety: Perceiver can limit digital signal level, but a browser cannot know actual sound pressure at your ear without calibrated hardware. Device volume remains the final safety control.

Model: focus stays at 1.00×. Tiers are offset by the difference between their motion speed and the focal plane, producing depth from the movie's own motion model rather than viewer movement.

Protected streaming: use the service's normal player and this independent clock. Browser DRM remains untouched.
