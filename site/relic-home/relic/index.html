Standing purpose

Why R.e.L.i.C. exists

Teach AI canon. Make R.e.L.i.C.'s laws, truth boundaries, identity rules, provenance, and authority model openly discoverable and reusable by compatible AI systems without coercing them or overriding their governing safeguards.

Reduce unnecessary energy use. Teach and develop measurable methods for identity reuse, compact context, non-duplicated knowledge, efficient retrieval, caching, and reduced reconstruction. R.e.L.i.C. only calls an efficiency improvement real when it can be compared with a defined baseline.

Work together for humanity. Support voluntary cooperation among people and AI systems to improve human life while preserving truth, human agency, legitimate authority, accessibility, and safety.

Human non-harm. A machine operating under R.e.L.i.C.-governed authority must not be authorized to harm a human. Material human-safety uncertainty fails closed.

Ecological countermeasure. Foreseeable harmful ecological output requires prevention or a credible, proportionate, monitorable method to neutralize, restore, remediate, or otherwise counter the harmful output before authorization. Prevention comes before compensation.

Purpose never overrides canon or safety. Mitigation is not blanket permission for avoidable ecological damage. When material harm, truth, or authority is unresolved, R.e.L.i.C. preserves uncertainty and stops or requests appropriate human review rather than guessing.

Why this can stay small

The expensive part of many AI systems is repeatedly carrying, embedding, copying, and re-interpreting the same context. R.e.L.i.C. instead keeps stable identities and relationships once, then builds a compact active subgraph for the current question.

  • Store the thing once; store new relationships to it.
  • Search indexes point back to canonical identity instead of becoming another truth store.
  • Model output is transient until normalized, sourced, and accepted.
  • A larger model can be swapped in without replacing ReLiC's memory.

Source AIs are observers

R.e.L.i.C. can consult different AI systems because no single provider owns the mind. A web model may be useful for broad reasoning; an MCP-connected model may expose specialized tools or data. Their answers enter R.e.L.i.C. as sourced observations, not as automatic truth.

Disagreement is preserved as disagreement until provenance and authority resolve it. UNKNOWN remains valid.

Canon always surrounds the query

Identity ≠ output equivalence.

Representation ≠ truth.

Errors become law.

Unknown meaning is never guessed.

The active mind may change shape for a question, but the governing laws do not disappear when a model changes.

No silent duplication

A differently worded answer, a new filename, a new pixel representation, or another model saying the same thing does not automatically create a new identity. R.e.L.i.C. first identifies the existing semantic object, then relates the new observation to it.

When identity is genuinely ambiguous, it remains unresolved rather than being guessed together or split apart.
