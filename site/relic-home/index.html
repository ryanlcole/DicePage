The Studio

ReLiCGameMaster

ReLiCGameMaster is creating a digital tabletop ecosystem where maps, dice, cards, miniatures, character sheets, terrain, worlds, rules, and stories can live in the same space while retaining the recognizable language of tabletop play.

The ambition is larger than reproducing a physical table on a screen: give players somewhere to play, GameMasters somewhere to create, storytellers somewhere to build continuity, artists somewhere their work can become playable, and developers a foundation that can grow without forcing every game into the same shape.

The Game · The World

Shaelvien

A Core Rule TTRPG built for a world that can keep growing.

Shaelvien can be played as its own tabletop roleplaying game. In MMO Mode, its Core Rules become the shared world's enforced rules of play, allowing many players and GameMasters to participate without every table becoming a different reality. Characters, places, encounters, time, and consequences can persist beyond a single session.

The Platform

RIST

Recursive Immersive Sandbox Table Top

RIST is the tabletop environment beneath Shaelvien: a recursive play and creation space for worlds within worlds, from global travel to tactical encounters and the objects inside a character's pack. It brings maps, miniatures, cards, dice, terrain, media, player tools, and GameMaster creation into one extensible table.

The Open Table

Your Game

Use the tools without surrendering the table.

Outside Shaelvien's MMO Mode, RIST is designed as a flexible tabletop studio rather than a mandatory rules engine. Build with its tools, bring a system your group already plays, create your own, or use the table as a foundation for entirely different games and interactive worlds.
