The Company

ReLiCGameMaster

ReLiCGameMaster is building a digital tabletop studio where the familiar pieces of tabletop play — maps, dice, cards, tokens, miniatures, character sheets, terrain, and imagination — can work together without forcing every game into the same shape.

The goal is straightforward: make creating, running, sharing, and playing worlds feel natural whether a group is gathered around a physical table or connecting online.

The Engine

RIST

Recursive Immersive Sandbox Table Top

RIST is the tabletop environment beneath Shaelvien: a recursive play space designed around maps, nested locations, physical-style game pieces, player tools, GM creation, and persistent worlds.

The World

Shaelvien

A world built to keep growing

Shaelvien is the public world built on RIST. Game Masters can create within the shared setting while players explore, build histories, and carry their progress forward through a persistent world.

The Table

Your Game

Tools before restrictions

RIST is being designed as a tabletop studio rather than a single ruleset. The table provides the pieces and controls; groups decide how they use them.
