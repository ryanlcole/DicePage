Immune Error Signal

Can molecular signals associated with abnormal/stressed-cell visibility and danger context help explain when cytotoxic immune activity appears stronger in tumors? This public experiment tests that narrow association against existing cancer data without touching a patient, cell, or treatment.

PUBLIC · REPRODUCIBLE · NON-INTERVENTIONAL

First public run · 2026-10-03 UTC

Observed association

443 melanoma RNA-seq samples were retrieved from the TCGA PanCancer Atlas study through cBioPortal.

visibility + danger vs cytotoxic activity: ρ = 0.683 · bootstrap 95% CI [0.623, 0.732] · FDR q = 8.57×10⁻⁶²

High visibility+danger quartile median cytotoxic score: 0.890; low quartile: -0.957; Mann-Whitney p = 6.96×10⁻³².

Critical confound signal: the immune-evasion signature correlated even more strongly with cytotoxic activity (ρ = 0.891). That makes immune infiltration/shared inflammatory regulation a serious alternative explanation. This run therefore supports an association in this cohort, not the proposed mechanism and not a treatment.

The hypothesis

anomaly visibility + danger context → stronger cytotoxic immune association?

The screen uses TCGA PanCancer Atlas melanoma RNA-seq exposed through cBioPortal. It asks whether a predefined expression signature for immune-visible stress/anomaly signals correlates with a predefined cytotoxic-immune signature.

Visibility signals

  • MICA, MICB, ULBP1/2/3
  • HLA-A/B/C and B2M
  • CALR, HMGB1, IFNB1 and CXCL10 as danger-context expression proxies

Immune readout

  • CD8A
  • NKG7 and GNLY
  • GZMB and PRF1
  • IFNG

The analysis also records an immune-evasion signature using CD274, IDO1 and LGALS9.

What a positive result would not mean

It would not establish that the proposed signals caused immune recognition, that changing them would treat cancer, or that a cure has been found. Bulk RNA-seq mixes tumor and immune cells. CALR/HMGB1 transcript abundance also does not prove immunogenic-cell-death signaling. A useful association would be a reason for independent researchers to test a more specific hypothesis, not a treatment instruction.

Open method

The public GitHub Actions job queries cBioPortal read-only, calculates standardized expression signatures, reports Spearman correlations with bootstrap confidence intervals, applies false-discovery-rate correction to the planned comparisons, and publishes the exact result bundle as a workflow artifact.

ReLiC classification: HYPOTHESIS until evidence is produced; observational association if reproduced; never promoted to therapeutic fact without appropriate biological and clinical evidence.
